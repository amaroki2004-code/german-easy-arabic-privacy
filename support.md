# Support – German Easy (Arabic)

For any question, problem or suggestion, email us at **amaroki2004@yahoo.com**. We usually reply within 48 hours.

## FAQ

**Is the app free?** Unit 1 is completely free. The German Easy Pro subscription unlocks all other units, games and the Word Bank.

**How do I cancel my subscription?** Settings → your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → German Easy → Cancel Subscription.

**I changed phones and lost my subscription.** Open the subscription screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

**I can't hear any audio.** Make sure your phone is not on silent mode and the volume is up.
